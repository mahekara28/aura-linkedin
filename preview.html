GETTING STARTED

Set up Aura in Chrome.

Aura is a local Chrome extension. Add it in four steps.

  1. Unzip your download.Extract aura-linkedin.zip, or use the provided aura-linkedin folder.
  2. Open Chrome's extensions page.Enter chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Load your extension.Click Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Choose your theme.Refresh LinkedIn, pin Aura from the puzzle menu, and select a theme.
Updating Aura? Replace the loaded files, click Reload on its extension card, then refresh LinkedIn. Your saved selection and preferences are kept.
